Parking

There is a paid, secure public car park directly next to the main entrance on West Derby Road. Parking is £5-£8 per day.

On-street parking is limited. Please respect local residents and do not park in areas marked as residential parking zones.

Parkopedia - Olympia Parking Info

By Train

The closest station to the venue is Liverpool Lime Street, which is about one mile away.

Connecting bus routes from the city centre pass through Queens Square Bus Station, directly opposite Lime Street Station.

There are Hackney taxi ranks outside both entrances to Lime Street Station.

By Bus

Several bus routes from the city centre stop approximately 100 metres from the venue's main entrance. The closest routes are 12, 13, 5 and 18.

These buses stop at both Liverpool ONE Bus Station on Paradise Street and Queens Square Bus Station, opposite Lime Street Station.

Check Merseytravel bus timetables before you travel.

By Taxi

Local taxi companies can pick up or drop off at the venue:

  • Alpha – 0151 722 8888
  • Excel – 0151 728 8888
  • Delta – 0151 924 7373

On event nights, there is often a Hackney taxi rank directly outside the venue's main entrance on West Derby Road.
