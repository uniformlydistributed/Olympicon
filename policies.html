EVENT INFORMATION

Event Policies

Code of Conduct

Olympicon is for everyone. Treat attendees, exhibitors, venue staff and organisers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted contact and disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Follow reasonable instructions from event staff and respect other people’s boundaries and property.

If you experience or witness a problem, speak to an Olympicon organiser or venue staff member as soon as you can. We will take concerns seriously and may ask someone to stop their behavior or leave the event where appropriate.

Photography

We may take official photographs at Olympicon. Before taking or sharing a photograph that identifies you, we will ask for your permission. You can decline or ask us to stop at any time, and we will respect your choice.

Ticket Refunds

For questions or requests about ticket refunds, please contact Liverpool Olympia directly using the contact details on its official Olympicon event page. Any refund will be subject to the terms that apply to your ticket.
