FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is Olympicon?

Join us on Saturday 23 January 2027 at Liverpool Olympia. Doors open at 10am, and the event runs until midnight.

Do I need to know how to play board games?

No experience is needed. Olympicon welcomes seasoned hobby gamers and people who are new to tabletop gaming. Join a demonstration to learn a new game.

I'm new to conventions or feeling nervous. What should I expect?

You don't need to know the games or bring a group. Take your time, browse the library, and join a demonstration if you'd like to learn a game. If you have a question before the event, contact us.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. You can choose a game from the library or join a demonstration. If you'd like advice before you arrive, get in touch with the team.

What games can I play?

There will be over 50 games in the games library. You can also bring your own games for open play.

Want to be an exhibitor?

Got games or other crafts to sell? Join us as an exhibitor! Find out more on our vendors page.

Where can I get tickets?

Liverpool Olympia uses Eventim as its exclusive ticket provider. Tickets are available through the Eventim booking page (opens in a new tab). Child tickets are available for under-12s, and under-6s enter free. Everyone under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. Check the event listing for current prices and availability.

Where can I find accessibility information?

For accessibility arrangements and visitor information, visit the Liverpool Olympia access information page.
