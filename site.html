FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is Olympicon?

Join us on Saturday 23 January 2027 at Liverpool Olympia. The event runs from 9am to 6pm, with last entry at 4:30pm.

Do I need to know how to play board games?

No experience is needed. Olympicon welcomes seasoned hobby gamers and people who are new to tabletop gaming. Join a demonstration to learn a new game.

What games can I play?

There will be over 50 games in the games library. You can also bring your own games for open play.

Can I bring games to sell?

Yes. Bring your own games to sell at the Bring and Buy, or browse to find a game to take home. Further details will be announced closer to the event.

Where can I get tickets?

Tickets are available through the Liverpool Olympia website. Early Bird and child tickets are available; check the event listing for current prices and availability.

Where can I find accessibility information?

For accessibility arrangements and visitor information, visit the Liverpool Olympia access information page.
