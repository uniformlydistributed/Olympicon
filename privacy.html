Privacy Notice

Who is responsible?

The Olympicon organisers are responsible for personal information submitted through our contact and exhibitor enquiry forms. For privacy questions, please use our contact form.

What we collect and why

The contact form collects your name, email address and message so we can respond. The exhibitor form may also collect your business or stall name, phone number, product details, links and setup requirements so we can consider and follow up on your enquiry.

We use this information to answer your request and, for exhibitor enquiries, to discuss possible stall arrangements. We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and managing event arrangements initiated by you. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Who receives your information?

Form submissions are handled by FormSubmit and delivered to the relevant Olympicon email inbox. The information is available to the organisers who handle enquiries and may be processed by the service providers needed to operate the form and email.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting arrangements, or to meet legal obligations, then delete it.

Tickets purchased through EVENTIM.Light

Ticket purchases made through EVENTIM.Light are handled separately from Olympicon. EVENTIM.Light is responsible for the personal information you provide during the ticket purchase process, under its own privacy policy. This is separate from Olympicon's privacy notice, which covers information submitted to our contact and exhibitor enquiry forms.

Only include information relevant to your enquiry in our forms. For information about data collected during ticket purchases, please refer to EVENTIM.Light's privacy policy.
